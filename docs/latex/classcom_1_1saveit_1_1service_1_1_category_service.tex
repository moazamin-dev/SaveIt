\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Service class providing business logic for managing categories. 

This class acts as an intermediary between the UI/\+Controller layers and the \doxylink{classcom_1_1saveit_1_1dao_1_1_category_d_a_o}{Category\+DAO}. It handles the creation, deletion, and resolution of categories specifically for an authenticated user.
